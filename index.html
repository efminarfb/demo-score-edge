01Why students stay

The things that actually move a band score

  • 01

    Your writing gets marked, not just collected

    Two full essays a week, marked against the four IELTS criteria, with the band written on the page and one thing to fix before the next class.

  • 02

    Twelve students, never more

    Speaking practice needs silence around it. With thirty students in a room, half of them never open their mouths.

  • 03

    We tell you the honest timeline

    Most students gain about half a band every ten weeks of steady work. If you need a full band by March, we will map out what that costs in hours.

  • 04

    A mock test every Saturday, under exam timing

    Full listening, reading and writing under the clock, then a review on Sunday. It is uncomfortable, which is the point.

05How it works

Four steps, about two weeks

  1. Take the level test

    About ten minutes online. Grammar, vocabulary and reading. You get an approximate level, not an IELTS band.

  2. Sit with a teacher

    Twenty minutes, free, in person or on a call. We look at your writing sample and your exam date.

  3. Join a batch

    Batches start on the first Saturday of every month. If the next one is full, we say so instead of squeezing you in.

  4. Sit the exam

    We book your test slot with you and run a full mock the week before. You keep all your marked essays.

She ___ to work by bus every day.

Correct. Present simple, third person, so the verb takes an s.

Not quite. The answer is goes. Present simple, third person.

Hardly ___ the room when the phone rang.

Correct. After a negative adverb like hardly, the subject and auxiliary swap.

Not quite. The answer is had he entered. Negative adverbs at the start force the inversion.

07Questions

Things people ask on the phone

What band do I need for Canada or the UK?
Most Canadian postgraduate courses ask for 6.5 overall with no band under 6.0. UK student visas usually want 5.5 to 6.0 depending on the institution. Nursing and medicine are higher. Bring your offer letter and we will read the exact wording with you.
Can I join in the middle of a batch?
Usually no, because the writing work builds week on week and you would be catching up from day one. If you are already around 6.0 and the batch is in week two, we will assess you and decide honestly.
What if I miss classes?
Two make-up sessions are included. After that you can sit in on another batch at the same level, space permitting. Missed mock tests can be taken on a weekday evening.
Do you refund the fee?
Full refund if you cancel before the second class. After that, half the fee back up to week three. Nothing after week three, because your seat and marking time are already committed.
